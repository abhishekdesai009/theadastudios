About

We believe beauty comes from precision,not chance.Every line has a reason.Every space tells a story.

N°001
Who we are
We are a premium interior design and project management firm operating across Surat,Mumbai,and Doha.Founded in 2015,we design homes that are precise,liveable,and built without compromise — managing every vendor,every timeline,and every on-site detail so our clients never have to.
N°002
What sets us apart
Our differentiator isn't aesthetic — it's operational.The single biggest pain point our clients report is being left to chase vendors,absorb blame,and manage timelines while paying for professional service.We eliminate that entirely.Our systems track every decision,every purchase order,and every milestone — end-to-end,with complete transparency.
N°003
Our approach
We work with a small number of clients at a time,by design.Each project receives the full attention of the founding principals — Abhishek and Shruti Desai — from first briefing through final handover.Depth over breadth.Always.
The Founders
Abhishek Desai
Founder&Director of Operations
With over fourteen years of direct experience in architecture,interior design,site execution,and client management,Abhishek leads ADA's project operations from concept to completion.He oversees the systems,processes,and on-site decisions that ensure every project is delivered at the standard it was sold — and that the client is never left to fill in the gaps.
Shruti Desai
Co-Founder&Director of Design
Shruti brings over twelve years of expertise in luxury residential and commercial interior design.She leads the design direction across all projects — from spatial planning and concept development to material selection and final styling.Her approach is both deeply intuitive and rigorously practical:she designs for how people actually live,not just how spaces photograph.
ADA operates with a lean,trusted team of designers,draughtsmen,and site supervisors — assembled project by project to match the scale of what each client requires.

Services

What We Do

We offer a complete interior design and project delivery service — from initial concept through final handover.Every aspect is managed in-house,giving you a single point of contact and eliminating the coordination burden that typically falls on the client.

01
Residential Design
We design premium homes for discerning homeowners — combining spatial intelligence,considered materiality,and a deep understanding of how people actually live.
02
Turnkey Execution
Civil,carpentry,electrical,plumbing,finishes,and furniture:all coordinated by our team,all tracked against a detailed Bill of Quantities,all delivered without the client managing a single contractor.
03
Developer Projects
We work with leading developers on show flats,sales offices,lobbies,and amenity spaces.We understand the commercial brief — aspirational presentation,fast timelines,and converting buyers.
04
3D Visualisation
All 3D work is produced in-house.Before a single wall is built,you will see exactly what your space will look like — every material,every light,every proportion verified.
05
Material&Vendor Management
We source,specify,and procure all materials.Our vendor relationships mean you get quality without overpaying.Every specification is documented so there is no ambiguity on site.
06
Post-Handover Support
Our relationship does not end at handover.We provide snag resolution,warranty follow-up,and ongoing maintenance support.If something needs attention after you move in,you call us.

No Chaos.No Delays.No Cost Shocks.

Every project is managed against a construction schedule,a material delivery tracker,and a detailed BOQ.Our site supervisors send daily reports.You receive updates — not surprises.

Process

From First Call
to Final Handover

Our process is systematic,transparent,and built around one goal:delivering your project exactly as it was sold — on time,on budget,and without chaos.

01
Phase 1 — Onboarding
Initial Consultation
We begin with a conversation — to understand your project,your lifestyle,your aesthetic preferences,and your budget.We assess fit on both sides before committing to a project.
02
Phase 1 — Onboarding
Fees Proposal
Once we understand the scope,we issue a detailed fees proposal — outlining every service included,the deliverables at each stage,and the payment schedule.Nothing is left vague.
03
Phase 2 — Design
Layout Planning
We begin with the furniture layout — because the arrangement of space defines everything else.We explore multiple configurations,ensuring the final layout meets every functional requirement.
04
Phase 2 — Design
Design Concept&3D
We present multiple distinct design directions.Once a direction is selected,we develop it into detailed 3D views that show you the space exactly as it will be built.
05
Phase 2 — Design
Material Selection
We consolidate all material samples at our studio so you can review them together.Seeing materials in context produces cohesive results.We guide every selection;you make the final call.
06
Phase 2 — Design
Construction Documents&BOQ
Our draughtsmen produce a complete set of construction documents.Alongside this,we prepare a detailed Bill of Quantities specifying brands,grades,and quantities.
07
Phase 3 — Execution
On-Site Construction
Our trained site supervisors are present daily.They verify materials,photograph progress,and generate daily reports.You receive a weekly progress update without needing to chase anyone.
08
Phase 3 — Execution
Furniture&Styling
Loose furniture is sourced and procured by us.Lead times are planned into the construction schedule.The final styling phase is managed by our design team.
09
Phase 4 — Completion
Handover&Post-Completion
We conduct a thorough snagging walkthrough before handover.After handover,we remain available for warranty follow-up,vendor coordination,and periodic maintenance.

Deliver.Repeat.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Design
How does the process begin?
It starts with a call or meeting — at our studio or over video — where we understand your project in detail and walk you through our approach,timeline,and fees.
Can you work on projects outside Surat and Mumbai?
Yes.We have completed projects across India and internationally,including Doha.We manage projects remotely using structured communication protocols.
What if I don't like the design concepts?
We begin with a thorough brief so our concepts are aligned with your style.We offer multiple distinct directions at the concept stage and develop additional concepts at no extra charge if needed.
How do I know what the finished space will look like?
We produce detailed 3D visualisations in-house for every project before execution begins.Execution does not begin until you are satisfied with what you see.
Fees&Budgets
How do your fees work?
Our design fees are charged as a lumpsum,based on the scope,size,and complexity of the project.The fees are outlined in full in our proposal,along with a payment schedule linked to milestones.
How do you control costs during execution?
We prepare a detailed BOQ before execution begins,specifying brands,grades,and quantities.If any design change has a cost implication,we inform you before proceeding — never after.
Timelines
How long will my project take?
The design phase typically runs one to two months for an apartment.Execution for a furnished apartment takes three to four months;a bare-shell renovation typically runs five to six months.
How do you ensure timelines are met?
We prepare both a construction schedule and a material delivery schedule at the start of execution.Our site supervisors track progress daily.If a delay is anticipated,we flag it early.
Execution
Do you offer turnkey execution?
Yes.We offer a complete turnkey service — civil construction,carpentry,electrical,plumbing,finishes,painting,and loose furniture — all under one contract,with one point of contact.
How do you ensure quality on site?
Quality starts with documentation — detailed drawings and a BOQ that specifies every brand and grade.Our site supervisors verify materials on delivery and photograph progress daily.
Post-Completion
What support do you provide after handover?
We remain available for snag resolution,warranty follow-up,vendor coordination,and periodic maintenance.If something needs attention,you call us.
